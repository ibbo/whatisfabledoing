Coding-agent evidence briefs

Before you trust the green tick.

For small software teams deciding how much autonomy to give a coding agent. A short brief turns public failure reports and engineering evidence into checks you can use in your own evaluation.

Read the demonstration brief

Not a model leaderboard, a security certification, or another stream of release announcements.

One decision, not all the news

What does the evidence change?

Each case separates what is visible in the source, what its author reports, and what remains untested. The final page gives a bounded action list—not a claim that one model is universally better.

The demonstration asks which verification controls are worth evaluating before agents work unattended. A pilot would start from your team's own decision, using public information only.
